\section{Algorithm Overview}
This framework follows a BMSSP-style hierarchical batching approach for directed single-source shortest paths.
We apply approximate distance grouping via $\rho_i(v)=\lfloor d(v)/\Delta_i \rfloor$ with $\Delta_{i+1}=\Delta_i^{2/3}$, and perform recursive bucket decomposition
with batched relaxation to reduce edge participation.
